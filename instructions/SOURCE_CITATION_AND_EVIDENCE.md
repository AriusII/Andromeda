# Source Citation and Evidence Standard

## Purpose

Keep Andromeda outputs auditable.

## Requirements

Every research-backed output must include:

- Source name.
- URL or file path.
- Date accessed, when available.
- Claim supported by the source.
- Confidence level.
- Whether the claim is stable, recent, preview, or experimental.

## Evidence classes

| Class        | Meaning                                                  |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Canonical    | Project doctrine or formal specification.                |
| Official     | Vendor or standards body documentation.                  |
| Academic     | Peer-reviewed or recognized research publication.        |
| Industry     | Vendor engineering article or reliable technical report. |
| Experimental | Preview feature, blog claim, or emerging practice.       |

## Rule

Never promote experimental AI tooling behavior into Andromeda doctrine without a decision record.
